\section{The whole model on one page}

MindFrame reads one thought into a vector $e\in[0,1]^9$ of nine movers (fear, sadness, anger, shame, calm, connection, hope, rumination, avoidance) and takes one gentle step toward a home state $h$. Every feature built so far fits in four lines:
\begin{align}
e &= \clip\!\big(e_{-} \oplus n(\text{text}) + X\,(1-S)\,w\big) && \text{the reading}\label{eq:read}\\
e' &= \clip\!\big(e + a(e)\,(h-e) + b\,s(e)\big) && \text{the step}\label{eq:step}\\
P &= P_d(e)\,(1-\gamma C)\,(1-\delta G);\ \ \ell=\arg\max_i \Delta P_i;\ \ \text{step}=\text{practice}(\ell) && \text{peace}\label{eq:peace}\\
\text{words} &= \text{compose}(e, e', P, \ell, \text{passes}). && \label{eq:words}
\end{align}
\begin{itemize}
\item $e_{-}$ is where the person was: the session's $e$, or the vector picked with the feeling chips. $n(\text{text})$ is what the words add: a lexicon, thinking-trap signals, and word hooks (relationship patterns, forgiveness cues, true size, patience, body tension), each saturating as $1-e^{-\text{raw}}$. The saturating sum $\oplus$ is $1-(1-e_j)(1-n_j)$ for hard and process movers and $e_j+n_j^{+}(1-e_j)-n_j^{-}e_j$ for the grounding movers (calm, connection, hope).
\item $X(1-S)w$ is the Kai term (Section~\ref{sec:kai}). For almost every text $X=0$, and line~(\ref{eq:read}) is just $e_{-}\oplus n$.
\item $a(e)=\min(a_{\max}, a_0+k\norm{e-h})$, lowered to $a_{\mathrm{cap}}=0.12$ (with $b=b_U$) when the active hard movers feed each other ($\lambda>0$). The term $s_j(e)=e_j\sum_{g}\min(0,W_{gj})\,h_g\le 0$ is grounding pressing on a hard or process mover that sits above home.
\item $P_d=1/(1+(d/d_0)^2)$ with $d=\norm{e-h}$. $C$ is the strongest hidden conflict $\sqrt{x_iy_j}$. $G=\max(g,u)$ is the true-size signal (too big, $g$; too small, $u$).
\item $\Delta P_i$ is the gain in $P$ when $e_i$ moves $0.05$ toward $h_i$. The lever $\ell$ is the largest gain. $\text{practice}(\ell)$ is a fixed, deterministic rule table: for example, the cold-water reset only for very high fear or anger with low peace, and the brain dump for very high rumination. Strictly, the step is the practice for the lever that maximizes $\Delta P$; the model does not search every practice.
\item The \emph{passes} run in the same call and never move $e$. They are forgiveness ($F_{\text{self}}=e_{\text{shame}}(0.4+0.6c_{\text{self}})$ and $F_{\text{other}}=e_{\text{anger}}(0.4+0.6c_{\text{other}})$), the freeing mix ($\max J=P-\mu N$ over small band moves), the Kai lines, and the companion. The companion grows only from caring actions (an Update, a step tried, a journal save, a practice finished), never from $e$.
\item Values are rounded to $0.01$ after~(\ref{eq:read}) and after~(\ref{eq:step}), as the code does. The baseline $B_t=0.8B_{t-1}+0.2e_t$ and the recovery count (repeat~(\ref{eq:step}) until $d<0.3$) reuse the same map.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Code and formula agree.} A unit test recomputes (\ref{eq:read})--(\ref{eq:peace}) from these lines alone and checks that \texttt{master()} returns exactly the same $e$, $e'$, $P$, lever and practice. The refactor changed no other behavior: all earlier tests still pass.

\section{The Kai equation: steadiness under outside pressure}\label{sec:kai}
In Ryan's story, Kai faces a storm of criticism. He anchors in self-awareness, applies forbearance and empathy, and his respect guard blocks the barbs without harming anyone, so impulsive reaction ``flickers and fails.'' The model adds no movers:
\begin{align*}
X &= 1-e^{-0.7k_X} && \text{hurt received (aimed \emph{at} you)}\\
A &= (1-G)\,[1-(1-c_A)(1-\tfrac12\sigma_A)] && \text{self-awareness}\\
F &= 1-(1-c_F)(1-\tfrac12\sigma_F)(1-p_F) && \text{forbearance}\\
E &= 1-(1-c_E)(1-\tfrac12\sigma_E) && \text{empathy}\\
R &= (1-c_{\text{attack}})\,[1-(1-c_R)(1-\tfrac12\sigma_R)] && \text{respect guard}\\
S &= \min\!\big(0.99,\ 1-(1-A)(1-F)(1-E)(1-R)\big) && \text{the shield}\\
e_j &\leftarrow \clip\!\big(e_j + X(1-S)\,w_j\big),\ \ w=(0.8,\,0.6,\,0.4)\text{ for anger, shame, fear} && \text{the push}\\
I &= X(1-S)\,u,\quad u=\max(e_{\text{anger}}, c_{\text{urge}}) && \text{impulsive reaction}
\end{align*}
Here each $c=1-e^{-0.7k}$ counts $k$ matching phrases, with negation handled (``nobody judged me'' gives $X=0$). $\sigma=1$ when a matching practice was finished this session: the pause or breathing practices for $F$, labeling for $A$, and the REACH ``Empathize'' step for $E$. Sessions are kept in memory only. $p_F=\tfrac12$ comes from a picker chip. Each virtue is capped at $0.95$. The Four Horsemen \citep{gottman1992} in the person's \emph{own} words never raise $X$; contempt or criticism aimed outward lowers $R$ through $c_{\text{attack}}$.

\textbf{On the card} (words only, and only when $X>0$), for the reading ``My coworkers criticized me in front of everyone, I want to snap back but I know they're stressed'': $X=0.75$, $A=0$, $F=0.5$, $E=0.5$, $R=0$, $S=0.75$, $I=0.14$. The card says: ``Your steadiness: self-awareness just starting, forbearance growing, empathy growing, respect guard just starting. The urge to react flickers and fails.'' The weakest virtue is the lever, and it comes with one step. For the respect guard, that step is DEAR MAN-style \citep{linehan2015}: ``I hear you. I see it differently, and I'm going to step away now.''

\textbf{Sources, honestly.} Naming feelings dampens amygdala response \citep{lieberman2007}; affirming core values buffers threat to the self \citep{cohen2014}. Patience relates to well-being and can be trained \citep{schnitker2012}. Perspective taking is a distinct facet of empathy \citep{davis1983}. Assertiveness training has a substantial, if older, evidence base \citep{speed2018}. None of these studies tested this equation. The product form of $S$, the weights $w$, and the $\tfrac12$ terms are modeling choices.

\section{A layered topology and the anchor}\label{sec:topology}
\paragraph{Layers.} Every variable in the MindFrame equations (102 in all, listed in \texttt{js/core/topology.js}) sits on one of six layers: L1 feelings and the step (25), L2 virtues and the Kai shield (14), L3 peace, conflict and true size (12), L4 grip, time and resistance (19), L5 past to future, hope, forgiveness, the freeing mix and the moving anchor (23), L6 faith and meaning (9, \emph{wording only}: the seed and the verse choice are not measured quantities). The state space is the product $E=L_1\times\cdots\times L_6$ over the base $L_1=[0,1]^9$. The upper layers are read from $L_1$ and the words by projection maps $\pi_2(e)=X(1-S)$, $\pi_3(e)=(P,C,G)$, $\pi_4(e)=(\text{fear},\text{loop},\text{vice},\rho)$, $\pi_5(e)=(\text{past},\text{hope})$, so a point over $e$ is the section $\sigma(e)=(e,\pi_2(e),\dots,\pi_6)$. We call this ``fiber-bundle style'' only in that loose sense. The companion's growth sits in L6 because it never depends on $e$. There is no daily score $\Psi$ in the current code, so none is listed.

\begin{center}\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{mf-anchor-layers.png}\end{center}

\begin{proposition}[The anchor]\label{prop:anchor}
Let $T(e)=\mathrm{clip}(e+a(e)(h-e)+b\,s(e))$ be master's step and $a_{\mathrm{lo}}=\min(a_0,a_{\max},a_{\mathrm{cap}})>0$. Then $\|T(e)-h\|\le q\|e-h\|$ with $q=1-a_{\mathrm{lo}}$ ($q=0.88$ with the defaults). Hence $h$ is the unique fixed point of $T$, every orbit from every point of $[0,1]^9$ converges to it (the basin is the whole cube), and $V(e)=\|e-h\|$ is a Lyapunov function, $V(T(e))\le qV(e)$ in the sense of \citet{lyapunov1992}.
\end{proposition}
\noindent\emph{Proof.} The inequality is Proposition~\ref{prop:contract}. If $T(e^\ast)=e^\ast$ then $\|e^\ast-h\|\le q\|e^\ast-h\|$ with $q<1$, so $e^\ast=h$. Iterating gives $\|T^t(e)-h\|\le q^t\|e-h\|\to0$. \qed

\noindent\textbf{Honest limit.} $T$ is \emph{not} a Banach contraction in the strict pairwise sense, $\|T(x)-T(y)\|\le q\|x-y\|$ for all $x,y$ \citep{banach1922}. The step size jumps to $a_{\mathrm{cap}}$ when the hard movers feed each other ($\lambda>0$), so $T$ is discontinuous there. Sampling 4000 pairs gives a pairwise ratio of up to 12.4, while the ratio toward $h$ never exceeds 0.871. The toward-a-point form above is what uniqueness and convergence need. Numerically, the app's rounded iteration from 500 random starts lands within 0.057 of $h$ in at most 21 steps. At the anchor, the layer coordinates are $L_1=h=(0,0,0,0,0.8,0.8,0.8,0,0)$, $\pi_2=0$, $\pi_3=(P,C,G)=(1,0,0)$, $\pi_4=(0,0,0,0)$ and $\pi_5=(0,0.8)$. With faith on, the anchor is described as rest in Christ: ``This hope we have as an anchor of the soul, a hope both sure and steadfast'' (Hebrews 6:19, WEB). The words change, not the math.

\paragraph{Time.} Each mover gets a time constant $\tau_i$ (hours; Table~\ref{tab:tau}). In continuous time, $\dot e_i=-(e_i-A_i)/\tau^{\mathrm{eff}}_i+\text{inputs}$, so over a gap $dt$, $e_i\mapsto A_i+(e_i-A_i)e^{-dt/\tau^{\mathrm{eff}}_i}$ with $\tau^{\mathrm{eff}}_i=\tau_i(1+e_{\mathrm{rum}})/(1-\rho)$. Rumination lengthens every $\tau$, as rumination goes with longer emotions \citep{verduyn2015}. The map contracts per gap with $k(dt)=\max_i e^{-dt/\tau^{\mathrm{eff}}_i}<1$ for every $dt>0$ and $\rho<1$. The app shows the time to come within 0.1 per mover, $t=\max_i\tau^{\mathrm{eff}}_i\ln(|e_i-A_i|/0.1)$, only as a wide range in words, never as a promise. The popular ``90-second'' rule for an emotion's chemical wave is not peer-reviewed and is not used.

\paragraph{Resistance.} Resistance $\rho\in[0,0.9]$ is built as $\rho=\min\!\big(0.9,\ [1-(1-0.4e_{\mathrm{avoid}})(1-0.5c_{\mathrm{sup}})(1-0.3e_{\mathrm{rum}})(1-0.3\,\iota)(1-0.3c_{\mathrm{sustain}})](1-0.5c_{\mathrm{will}})\big)$. Its parts:
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=0pt]
\item experiential avoidance \citep{hayes2006,bond2011};
\item suppression or fighting words, which tend to bring a thought back \citep{wegner1987};
\item rumination;
\item emotional inertia $\iota$, the lag-1 autocorrelation of the saved days \citep{kuppens2010};
\item sustain talk \citep{millerrollnick2013};
\item willingness and acceptance words, which lower $\rho$.
\end{itemize}
Resistance slows time ($\tau^{\mathrm{eff}}=\tau/(1-\rho)$) and weakens the pull ($a_{\mathrm{eff}}=a(1-\rho)$). The anchor step still contracts, with $q_\rho=1-a_{\mathrm{lo}}(1-\rho)\le0.995<1$. The signs are as intended: $\partial\rho/\partial c_{\mathrm{sup}}>0$, so pushing harder raises resistance, and $\partial\rho/\partial c_{\mathrm{will}}<0$, so willingness lowers it. The weights are modeling choices.

\paragraph{The moving anchor.} The anchor is a moving focus point, not one fixed point. It is updated as $A_k=\mathrm{clip}\big(\lambda A_{k-1}+(1-\lambda)(\beta e+(1-\beta)d)\big)$, with $\lambda=0.6$ and $\beta=0.25+0.25\rho$. Here $d$ is the desired feelings (the freeing mix, else $h$), and $A_{-1}=h$. Within an epoch $A_k$ is fixed, and $e\mapsto\mathrm{clip}(e+a_{\mathrm{eff}}(A_k-e))$ contracts toward it with $q_\rho$, so $A_k$ is that epoch's unique fixed point. A transition to $A_{k+1}$ (called ``collapse'' only as a picture; nothing quantum) happens when $\|e-A_k\|\le\varepsilon_{\mathrm{in}}=0.15$ has held for a dwell time $\tau_{\mathrm{dwell}}$. The dwell breaks only when $\|e-A_k\|>\varepsilon_{\mathrm{out}}=0.25$; this hysteresis prevents flip-flopping. With $d$ fixed, $\|A_{k+1}-d\|\le q_A\|A_k-d\|+(1-\lambda)\beta\varepsilon_{\mathrm{out}}$, with $q_A=\lambda+(1-\lambda)\beta\le0.79$. So the chain stays in the cube and approaches a ball of radius $\beta\varepsilon_{\mathrm{out}}/(1-\beta)\le0.23$ around $d$ geometrically. A stone is added only when $A$ moves by at least 0.02. A sample chain (sad, replaying, $\rho=0.40$, $d=(\dots,0.9,0.6,0.7,\dots)$ then $(\dots,0.7,0.9,0.8,\dots)$) runs calm/connection/hope $0.78/0.71/0.73\to0.81/0.68/0.72\to\cdots\to0.86/0.64/0.71$ by 71 h, then turns toward the new desire, reaching $0.72/0.87/0.79$ by 205 h. The path is kept on the device only.

\paragraph{Expression governor.} The goal is full expression up to a safe limit; the governor offers a gentle pause, never a block. It uses three signals:
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=0pt]
\item unawareness $U$: numb or detached words, flat repetition and low self-awareness;
\item irritability $Y$: anger rising per hour, and irritation words;
\item mania-like activation $M_n$: racing words, pressured long entries, true size $G$, short-sleep words, and bursts of entries.
\end{itemize}
From these it forms the activation $\alpha=1-(1-Y)(1-M_n)(1-\tfrac12U)$ and a limit $\ell(C)=0.45(1-0.5C)$, which tightens as conflict rises. A PI controller with leak and anti-windup \citep{astrom2008} runs on the error $E=\alpha-\ell(C)$: $I_k=\mathrm{clip}_{[-1,1]}(0.8I_{k-1}+E_k)$, frozen while $u$ is saturated, and $u_k=\mathrm{clip}_{[0,1]}(1.2E_k+0.4I_k)$. The output is graded: keep writing; a slow-down pause; breathe and ground (STOP; \citealp{linehan2015}; an early pause in the sense of \citealp{gross1998}); step away. \emph{Stability (honest scope):} $u$ and $I$ are bounded by the clips. The leak makes the controller state a contraction, $|I_k-I'_k|\le0.8|I_{k-1}-I'_{k-1}|$, so old error fades geometrically. The governor never feeds back into $e$, so $q$, $q_A$ and $q_\rho$ are untouched. The loop through the person is not modeled. If racing words or short sleep appear on three or more days, a gentle line suggests talking with a doctor. This is not a diagnosis; DSM-5 \citep{apa2013} is context only. Crisis wording still shows only the 988/911 box.

\paragraph{Scenes and About me.} Thirteen small original SVG scenes follow the parable's beats with the same traveler and fawn. The text and the pictures come from one list of beats, so they stay in sync. A grounding picture is chosen from the governor (activation or true-size drift: a shared table or a garden; irritability: open water or a horizon; unawareness: hands or a lamp) and is paired with one kind, concrete action. This draws on positive imagery \citep{holmes2010}, nature and attention restoration \citep{kaplan1995,berman2008} and kindness and well-being, a small average effect \citep{curry2018}. The optional, local-only \emph{About me} (hobbies, field) does four things: it adds a behavioral-activation step \citep{lewinsohn1974,martell2001}, a field motif and metaphor, one more song or film, and a word of room for flow \citep{csikszentmihalyi1990}. It never changes a number.

\section{What is proven, what is tested, what is modeled}
\begin{proposition}[Contraction still holds]\label{prop:contract}
Let $T$ be the step map~(\ref{eq:step}) with hard and process homes at $0$. Then for every $e\in[0,1]^9$, $\norm{T(e)-h}\le(1-a_{\mathrm{lo}})\norm{e-h}$ with $a_{\mathrm{lo}}=\min(a_0,a_{\max},a_{\mathrm{cap}})>0$. The Kai term does not change $T$, so the iteration converges to $e^\ast=h$, where $P=1$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
For a grounding mover, $T(e)_j-h_j=(1-a)(e_j-h_j)$. For a hard or process mover, $T(e)_j=\clip(e_j(1-a-b\kappa_j))$ with $\kappa_j=-\sum_g\min(0,W_{gj})h_g\ge0$, so $0\le T(e)_j\le(1-a)e_j$. Summing squares gives the bound. The Kai term enters only line~(\ref{eq:read}): it takes a point of $[0,1]^9$ to another point of $[0,1]^9$ (clipped), raising only anger, shame and fear by at most $X w_j$. The proof needs nothing else from the starting point, and the solve and recovery iterate $T$ with empty text, where $X=0$.
\end{proof}
\begin{remark}[If the pressure were applied at every step]
Suppose the map were instead $T_X(e)=\clip(T(e)+p)$ with a constant $p=X(1-S)w\ge0$ on the hard movers. Then $|T_X(e)_j-h_j|\le(1-a)|e_j-h_j|+p_j$, so $d_{t+1}\le(1-a_{\mathrm{lo}})d_t+\norm{p}$ and $\limsup d_t\le\norm{p}/a_{\mathrm{lo}}$. That is bounded, but it does \emph{not} converge to $h$. This bound is loose (with the defaults $a_{\mathrm{lo}}=0.12$). The app does not do this: outside hurt is applied once per reading.
\end{remark}
\begin{proposition}[The shield behaves as the story says]
$S\in[0,0.99]$ and $\partial S/\partial V=\prod_{U\neq V}(1-U)\ge0$ for each virtue $V$. With $X$ fixed, $X(1-S)$ and the pushed anger fall as $S$ rises, so $u$ and $I=X(1-S)u$ are non-increasing in $S$; $I$ is strictly decreasing when $X>0$ and $u>0$ (before rounding). With $X=0$ nothing changes: no push, $I=0$, and no card tile.
\end{proposition}
\noindent\textbf{Tested, not proven:} the code matches (\ref{eq:read})--(\ref{eq:peace}) to the cent. The rounded iteration stops within $0.005/a$ of $h$. The detection separates hurt received from hurt given on the listed examples. Crisis wording returns only the 988/911 box. Everything is deterministic. The unit suite has 299 checks. \textbf{Modeled, not validated:} the movers' links, every weight and threshold, the cue lists, the virtue formulas, and the practice rules. No study with people has been run.
