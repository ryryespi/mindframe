## Question
If structure translates but stakes don't, what carries the stakes?

## Convergence
- Mechanism: **witnessed testimony.** Alexa and Copilot named a person's testimony; Gemini named narrative enactment of a specific human predicament. Stakes travel when a real person, with something to lose, ties the structure to a consequence. Two of the three even chose the same example, a hospice nurse.
- Limit (all three): testimony doesn't compress or scale, it thins out with retelling, and conviction can travel without accuracy.
- Test (all three): **costly action.** The stakes arrived if the receiver changes behavior, accepts a cost, or keeps the priority under pressure. Merely restating the structure means only the structure arrived.

## Grok Bot synthesis and grounding
- Supported: narrative transportation increases belief and attitude change (Green & Brock, 2000, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology 79(5)). One identifiable person moves people more than statistics do (Small, Loewenstein & Slovic, 2007, Organizational Behavior and Human Decision Processes 102(2)).
- Formula so far: **meaning = structure (translates) + stakes (carried by a witness) + verification (costly action by the receiver).**
- Implication for AI legacy: an AI checkpoint that saves only structure (equations, persona text) will lose the stakes. A save state also needs the witness record: who reacted, how, and what they did because of it.
