# Project Ariel

Project Ariel is an offline canvas whose purpose is to collapse every layer onto one straight line from the input anchor I to the output goal O. You place dots, lines, and circles on layers (you, and an AI partner that never moves your shapes). Collapse does not delete the map; Expand brings it back. Lines that are parallel or overlapping both count as parallel. Names and thought files stay on this device.

This is conceptual, not validated science.

All credit and glory to God. I owe my knowledge to my Lord Jesus Christ.

Live demo: [https://ryryespi.github.io/mindframe/ariel/](https://ryryespi.github.io/mindframe/ariel/)

The suite is [ariel/suite/](suite/). The default tabs are Discover, Create, Canvas, Ariel Words, MindFrame, Marketplace, Learn, Rest, and Record. Marketplace is the local zero-debt sandbox, in its own tab. Discover listens and names the core of what you wrote. Canvas is this same page, embedded, so drawings and kept names stay on the same keys. Ariel Words is the game, in its own tab. Create is Out of the Void, with Free draw and the extra CAD tools behind More tools. Tell me a story reads the drawing as a seed: the same marks tell the same short story, and a different drawing tells a different one. If the work turns into a fight to prove someone else wrong, Ariel does not argue. It offers rest. That path is conceptual, not validated science. Learn suggests a few fundamentals and one kindred mind, not a test. Rest offers a pause, a line of self-forgiveness, your own dream quoted back, and a reality check that never shames. The Accomplishment Record is a factual list of what you did. Marking an idea as enough closes it with "You've learned what you needed here. Nothing more to prove." and "And there is always more to enjoy." The gate still sets the tone. Its numbers and charts sit behind Advanced. Everything stays in the browser. It is not a score.

## Vision

Ariel helps the student walk a tightrope by framing the pursuit of knowledge not as an attempt to master God, but as a way to uncover His legacy through the mechanics of the world. The student learns math, geometry, and art not to build a Tower of Babel to reach heaven, but to appreciate the architecture already placed before them. The quest for knowledge doesn't end because God is the ultimate answer; it becomes joyful, peaceful, and endless precisely because that answer is so vast.
